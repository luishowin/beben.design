## How engagements work

1. The client writes a few lines about the problem through /contact/.
2. A free discovery call covers goals, constraints and timeline.
3. A fixed-price scope with line items follows.
4. A 50% deposit starts the work.

Three ways to work together: Project (fixed price, 20 business days), Sprint
(10 business days, +$100, near-real-time feedback), and Retainer (a monthly
block of capped hours, quoted after the first project). The standard clock
pauses while waiting on client content or feedback.

Working hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 EAT. Luis works with
clients globally.

## The Labs

- The Labs at /labs/ collect the free tools, apps and experiments. The free
  tools run entirely in the browser: no accounts, no uploads, no data
  collection. Each one can be downloaded as a single HTML file that keeps
  working offline forever.
- Live now:
  - QR Code Generator (/qr-code-generator/): Make unlimited free QR codes with custom colors, patterns, frames, and logos. No sign-up, no watermark.
  - EXIF Viewer and Stripper (/exif-stripper/): See the GPS coordinates, camera serial, and timestamps hidden in a photo, then download a clean copy.
  - Checksum Verifier (/checksum-verifier/): Hash a downloaded file with SHA-1, SHA-256, SHA-384, or SHA-512 and compare it to the published value.
  - Text Diff (/text-diff/): Compare two revisions of a contract or a copy deck side by side, without uploading either one.
  - Contrast Grid (/contrast-grid/): Paste a whole palette, get every foreground and background pair scored against WCAG AA and AAA at once.
  - Dither Machine (/dither-machine/): Drop an image and push it through Atkinson, Floyd-Steinberg, or ordered Bayer dithering. Export the PNG.
  - Does It Fit? (/character-counter/): One text box, checked live against every limit that matters: title tags, meta descriptions, posts, subject lines.
  - Beben Arcade (/games/): Twenty-three tiny 8-bit games at one address. Add it to your home screen, play offline, beat your best. No ads, ever.
  - Corpus (/codex/): A small library you can carry: thirteen subjects in portable files, answers in milliseconds, no server needed. Formerly Codex, now v2.0.
  - Essential Watch (/essential-watch/): A calm, repairable wrist instrument between the Casio F-91W and the Apple Watch Ultra. Spec v6 in the open, with a live emulator.
  - Rev Log (/rev-log/): An open source telemetry system for motorcycles: lean, throttle, braking and revs captured on the bike, visualised in the browser, owned by the rider.
- In development: JWT Decoder, Passphrase Generator, Colour Blindness Simulator, SVG Cleaner, Favicon Forge, Fluid Type Calculator, Invoice Builder, Freelance Rate Calculator, Screenshot Framer, Barcode Generator.
