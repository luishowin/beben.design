What I do

Three ways to start

Websites

From $250 USD

For businesses, startups and campaigns that need to exist properly online.

Included

  • Design and build of a fast, responsive website, with the page count agreed in the quote.
  • Help securing your domain name, registered in your name.
  • A custom email address on Google Workspace, set up and tested.
  • Foundational SEO, so Google can find your site: titles, descriptions, a sitemap and Search Console setup.
  • Foundational branding: colour, type and tone that capture your personality on the site, not a full brand identity.
  • A defined number of revision rounds, agreed in the quote.

Timeline: 20 business days. A 10-business-day sprint is +$100.
Price: from $250. Larger scopes get a fixed-price quote with line items.

Brand identity

From $250 USD

For businesses starting from zero, or reworking what they already have.

Included

  • Logo.
  • Colours and typography.
  • Brand voice.
  • Print material: business cards and one flyer.
  • Clothing applications: a cap and a t-shirt.
  • A brand guide as a PDF, with editable source files.

When reworking an existing brand, I first work out which parts people already recognise and keep those.
Timeline: 20 business days. A 10-business-day sprint is +$100.
Price: from $250.

Product & UX design

Quoted

For products and software: web apps, mobile, desktop and hardware interfaces.

Choose what you need

  • UX research: interviews, journey maps, information architecture.
  • Wireframes and a clickable prototype.
  • Usability testing with prioritised findings.
  • UI design and a component library, with WCAG AA accessibility checks.
  • Developer handoff.
  • Product strategy and a discovery workshop.

Price: fixed, after a free discovery call.
